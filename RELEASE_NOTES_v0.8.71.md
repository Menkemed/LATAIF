# LATAIF v0.8.71

Partner balances are now visible in Receivables and Payables.

- **Receivables — new type "Partner":** what a partner still owes LATAIF for jointly bought items
  (open cost share, repayment after a corrected settlement). Shown with partner, purchase, item,
  origin and open amount.
- **Payables — new type "Partner":** what LATAIF still owes a partner ("Pay out").
- **Same numbers as the partner page:** the lines are the open balances the partner module already
  keeps — nothing is booked or paid automatically. A line disappears as soon as nothing is open
  (paid in, repaid, paid out, settled, offset). A click opens the item in the partner module.
- **No due date:** partner lines are shown as "Open" and never count as overdue.
- **Totals count every line once:** Receivables and Payables show customers/suppliers and partners
  separately. On the dashboard the card is now called "Customer Receivables"; partners are shown
  next to it, not added. The business report lists partners in their own block.
- Works the same on the second computer.

Update the main computer first, then the second computer. No data is changed by the update.
