# LATAIF v0.8.65

Jointly bought items at a glance in each partner card.

- **Totals per partner:** paid in, profit share, paid out and open — with who owes whom
  ("… owes LATAIF …", "LATAIF owes …" or "Settled — nothing open").
- **Running items in the card:** every item of this partner that still needs something, with its
  stage (in stock, sold · to settle, correction due, settled, repayment due), the open amount and
  the one matching action — Pays in, Repays, Pay out or Settle. The action opens the same window
  as in the item table below.
- **Done items** (settled or taken over by LATAIF) are counted and can be shown.

The detailed table of jointly bought items below stays as it was. Nothing in the bookings changes.

Update the main computer first, then the second computer. No data is changed by the update.
