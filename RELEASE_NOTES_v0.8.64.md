# LATAIF v0.8.64

Partner money on jointly bought items now follows what is actually open.

- **Contributions are limited to what the partner owes.** A partner can pay in their open cost
  share of unsold pieces, or pay back a claim after a return or correction — never more. On an
  item that is settled, taken over by LATAIF, or where LATAIF owes the partner, no contribution
  can be booked; the app says why. This is checked on the main computer, also for bookings made
  on the second computer.
- **One matching action per item** on the Partners page:
  - "Partner pays in" — the partner still owes their share of the cost.
  - "Partner repays" — the partner owes money back, also after the partnership on the item ended.
  - "Pay out" — LATAIF owes the partner.
  - Nothing open: "Settled — nothing open" or "Taken over by LATAIF — settled" instead of a button.
- The payment window shows the maximum amount. Earlier contributions, payouts and reversals stay
  visible in the history.

Payouts, settlement, take-over, partner change, supplier payments, VAT and profit are unchanged.

Update the main computer first, then the second computer. No data is changed by the update.
