# LATAIF v0.8.66

A complete purchase from the phone — booked on the main computer.

- **New Purchase on the phone:** the mobile page has a new "New Purchase" mode next to the
  existing Purchase Photo inbox.
- **Supplier:** pick an existing supplier, an existing customer or enter a new person.
  A customer who is already linked to a supplier keeps that supplier; otherwise the same person
  also becomes the supplier. A new person becomes a customer with a linked supplier. If a
  supplier that might be the same person already exists, the main computer asks first.
  Customer and supplier balances are never netted against each other.
- **Several items per purchase:** each one new or an existing item, as a single piece or with a
  quantity (no forced split). The category fields are the same as on the desktop.
- **Photos and AI Identify per item:** up to 8 photos per item. AI Identify only fills empty
  description fields — never prices, quantities, partners or payments.
- **Partner per item (optional):** only shown when active partners exist; share in % or BHD,
  rounded to 0.01 % as on the desktop.
- **Several payments:** cash, bank or Benefit, each with an optional reference. Whatever is not
  paid stays open with the supplier.
- **Draft, Waiting, Booked:** a purchase is kept on the phone as a draft, survives a restart or a
  lost connection and is only booked once the main computer confirms it. Supplier, customer,
  purchase, items, photos, partner shares and payments are booked together or not at all.
  Sending again never books twice.
- **Compact form:** foldable sections with a short summary each; a waiting or booked purchase is
  locked and shows only the summaries.

Purchases entered on the desktop work exactly as before.

Update the main computer first, then the second computer. No data is changed by the update.
