# LATAIF v0.8.112

A small display improvement for purchases. **Installing the update deletes nothing and changes no item, invoice, payment, booking or stock.**

## Purchase → "Variant of existing"

- When you choose the existing item, moving the mouse over an entry now shows the **item card** — photo, prices and details such as size, Karat & Color and weight — the same card as in the "Existing" item picker.
- Works in **New Purchase** and **Edit Purchase**.
- Only the display changes; choosing the item, the model, the photo and the booking work exactly as in v0.8.111.

**Database:** no change.

**Known limits:**
- **Second computer on v0.8.101:** unchanged from v0.8.111 — it does not show "Variant of existing", so it does not show this card either.
- **Unchanged from v0.8.111:** all known limits listed there still apply.

**Before you update:**

1. Stop entering anything on the phone, and wait until every phone upload has been processed on the main computer.
2. Check **Unresolved saves** on the second computer. Clarify open entries; do not remove them blindly.
3. Close both computers.
4. Make a full archive copy of the data folder (both databases, the complete media folder, the staging folders and the key files) on E: and F:, and check it.

**Update** the main computer. The second computer can stay on v0.8.101 for now.
