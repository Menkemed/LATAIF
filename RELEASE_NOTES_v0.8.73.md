# LATAIF v0.8.73

A new A5 invoice for printing and PDF, the same company details on both computers, and photos from
the phone gallery.

- **A5 invoice:** "PDF", "Print" and "Save & Print" on an invoice now print one A5 page (portrait)
  in the company layout: the emblem in the middle, the English name on the left, the Arabic name on
  the right, CR No. and VAT No. under each name.
- **Title by status:** a fully paid invoice prints as "TAX INVOICE", a partly paid one as "ADVANCE
  PAYMENT INVOICE" (with Paid and Balance Due), a draft as "PROFORMA INVOICE"; a cancelled invoice
  carries a "CANCELLED" stamp.
- **Items:** the name with the SKU small beside it, and below it the details as plain values.
  - Watches show Ref, Serial, case diameter, dial, bezel, material, karat & color and strap type.
  - Jewellery shows its type (Ring, Bangle …), Ref, Serial, Size, karat, weight and diamonds.
  - The condition is no longer printed.
- **Footer and terms:** amount in words, the margin-scheme notice, your invoice terms, two signature
  lines, and one footer line with address, phone, email and Instagram.
- **Settings → Company Information:** new fields for CR No., VAT No., Instagram and Invoice Terms.
  An empty field prints the value shown in grey.
- **Second computer:** every print takes the company details and payment methods from the main
  computer at that moment. A change in Settings shows on the next print on both computers.
- **Gold in names:** karat values read "18K Yellow Gold", "18K White Gold", "18K Rose Gold" and so
  on, wherever an item is named (invoice, collection, search, phone). Silver is unchanged; stored
  values are unchanged.
- **Names:** a jewellery type is no longer repeated when the description already says it ("Double
  Ring" instead of "Ring · Double Ring").
- **Phone — New Collection Item:** the photo area now offers "Take photo" and "Choose from
  gallery". Several gallery photos can be chosen at once. They are added to the photos already
  taken, up to 8 per item, and each can be removed. AI Identify and sending work exactly as with
  camera photos.

Update the main computer first, then the second computer. Reload the phone page once after the
update. No existing data is changed.
