# LATAIF v0.8.68

One stone list for Gold-Diamond Jewellery.

- **Stones:** a Gold-Diamond Jewellery item can now carry several stones — Diamond, Emerald,
  Sapphire, Ruby, Pearl, Moissanite, Cubic Zirconia, Amethyst, Aquamarine, Topaz, Tourmaline, Opal,
  Garnet, Onyx, Turquoise or Other (with a name). Each row has a quantity and an optional total
  carat; diamond rows can also have color, clarity and shape.
- **Diamond Weight is calculated** from the diamond rows with carat. A Diamond Weight saved before
  the stone list stays until diamond rows with carat replace it.
- **Everywhere:** collection (new and edit), purchase, consignment and repair, on the desktop and
  on the phone. The item view shows every stone and a short summary, e.g.
  "Diamond 0.80 ct · Emerald 0.45 ct · Tsavorite ×6"; stones are searchable.
- **AI Identify** suggests stones for gold, but never replaces stones that are already entered.
- **Collection card:** numbers with their unit — "6.40 g · 0.80 ct · Ring · 18K Yellow" instead of
  "6.4 · 0.8 · Ring · 18K Yellow".

Update the main computer first, then the second computer. Existing items are not changed; the
Gold-Diamond Jewellery category gets the new Stones field.
